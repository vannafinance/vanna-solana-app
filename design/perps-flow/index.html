Phoenix perps: first-time and returning flows

One sheet that walks a trader into Phoenix, in the motion language of the margin account flow (src/ui/first-time-flow.tsx). One wallet approval covers every part; the stage only moves once the wallet has signed. Every page is interactive: tap the sheet's buttons and answer in the mock Phantom wallet beside the phone.

First time

One action ("Opening long SOL 2x") with the normal progress bar. The motion tells the story without step labels: the Phoenix account card draws in, shimmers and fills in the centre; USDC flows in from the margin account on the left; the order fires at the market on the right and the position lands on the card. Plus the failures and the compact "Your USDC is in Phoenix" state with Retry order / Return USDC to margin.

Multi-leg transaction sheet

One approval, several transactions: Close all, Perps open in two parts, margin steps. A row per leg follows its real phases (Waiting to sign, Checking, Sending, Confirming, Done, Failed), the bar counts legs done, and there are finish, partial-failure and "still sending in the background" states. Interactive with a mock wallet and chain controls.
