Decisions

  • Built from the real sheet. Sheet shell, title row and close button from overlay.tsx; legs card, rows, progress bar with Sent, Processed, Confirmed, the done and failure layouts from tx.tsx; buttons from button.tsx with the Margin family gradient; colours from tokens.ts, light and dark.
  • The real mascot. TailLoader while a transaction is sending, MascotDone (tongue strike, happy eyes, then blinks) the moment the last one is confirmed. Ported from tail-loader.tsx, mascot.tsx and chameleon.ts.
  • Two paths. A new wallet opens its margin account first (one time). A wallet that already has one sees the app's normal review, with Health factor and Account leverage rows, and never a word about opening an account.
  • Transactions per chain. On Stellar each deposit and each borrow is its own transaction. On Solana the deposits travel together and the borrows travel together; with an existing account, deposit and borrow go in one transaction.
  • One transaction keeps the app's sheet. Loader, Sending, Sent to Stellar, the bar and dots, then the done face, with a lighter account strip and flow above. Several transactions add a stepper and resume from the failed one.
  • Value has a direction. Each deposit flows up from its wallet node and each borrow from its pool node. The strip counts the landing amount, then folds to a count such as "3 assets" and truncates rather than overlap.
  • Failure keeps the app's layout (pink ring, Not confirmed, Try again). With several transactions it lists each one and retries from the failed step.
