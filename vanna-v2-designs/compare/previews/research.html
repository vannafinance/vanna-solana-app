Research: Explore and Farm

What 12 finance and crypto apps put under the balance, and how 10 products design leveraged yield. Screenshots come from the public App Store listings and product docs (the Play Store pages could not be read by the tools). Tap any screenshot to open it full size.

Six patterns found

A
Your Vanna: one row per space, showing your state
Seen in Nexo, Cash App, Revolut

Earn "$2,677 supplied · +$1.37 earned", Trade "2 positions · +$210", Farm "health 2.00 · 5.03% on Kamino". If you have not used a space, its row becomes a one-line pitch with a real number. Replaces the tiles without repeating the top tabs.

B
One "next step" card, ranked
Seen in SpareCash, Monzo, Trust

Only the most useful thing right now: 1) a position close to liquidation (never dismissible), 2) idle money that could earn, 3) setup not finished, 4) a new market. Hidden when there is nothing to say.

C
Markets / movers list
Seen in Kraken, Coinbase, Jupiter

Repeats the Trade tab, and "top movers" next to 5x leverage nudges impulsive trades. Only acceptable as "your assets" (the stocks behind your health).

D
Quick actions row
Seen in Coinbase, Jupiter, Phantom, Revolut

Deposit / Withdraw / Swap / Add collateral. Useful, but still navigation: belongs in the header, not as the replacement.

E
Rewards / referral strip
Seen in DeFi App, Trust, Jupiter

Only if Vanna runs points or referrals. Secondary and dismissible.

F
Learn / risk card
Seen in Rainbow, Nexo

Good for leverage newcomers. Best as the new-user version of B, or a link on the Farm and Trade rows.

RECOMMENDATION

B + A: one "next step" card, then "Your Vanna" rows. Closest to Nexo, Cash App and SpareCash.

  1. Next step (only if there is something worth saying): risk first ("TSLAx long · health 1.25 · Add collateral"), then idle money ("1.25 TSLAx is idle · earn 1.82%, about $8/yr"), then setup, then a new market.
  2. Your Vanna: Earn, Trade, Farm rows with your live numbers; unused spaces show a one-line pitch with a real APY.
  3. Swap becomes a quick action in the header; movers stay in Trade; rewards only if Vanna launches points.

A new user sees: "Make your first deposit" + three pitch rows. Once they act, it becomes personal.

2 · Farm without the step-by-step form

Today Farm is a numbered form (pool → 1 deposit → 2 leverage → long table → profit). No strong product does it this way.

Kamino Multiply The venue Vanna uses

Strategy list, then one panel: amount + target leverage, with Net APY as the headline. One transaction. Manage: adjust leverage, collateral, debt, withdraw (full withdraw unwinds).

Source
Drift Amplify (now Velocity) Outcome and risk kept apart

Pick a pair, then one panel: amount + "Amplify ratio", then two short blocks: Position preview and Risk preview, then confirm.

Source
DeFi Saver Live preview, details at confirm

One amount field, and the new safety ratio and liquidation price update before you act. Route, slippage and fees appear only in the confirm step.

Source
Summer.fi Multiply Shut down after a July 2026 exploit

Collateral + one leverage slider labelled by meaning ("decrease risk / increase risk"), liquidation price and LTV live underneath, "current → after" values when adjusting.

Source
Morpho vault Money, not just %

Deposit input with simulated APY and projected earnings in $ per month and per year; details in tabs.

Leverage is a chip ("20x") that opens a sheet with a slider; the order preview shows margin and liquidation price.
Pendle
Two modes on the same product: Simple (one fixed-APY tile) and Pro.

What every good one has in common

  • No numbered steps. Pick a strategy → one panel (amount + leverage, result updating live) → a confirm sheet.
  • One headline number: net APY, also said in money ("≈ $63 a year").
  • Risk in one line, not a table: health and the liquidation point.
  • Rates, route, slippage, fees only at confirm.
  • Leverage as preset chips (or a chip that opens a slider sheet).
  • Managing reuses the same panel with "now → after" values.

Health = L ÷ (L − 1), which matches the app today (2.0x → 2.00). Your TSLA exposure stays 1× because the borrowed USDC goes to Kamino, not into more stock, so "5×" boosts the yield, not the stock bet. Please confirm both with engineering. The Safe / Moderate cut at 1.50 is a proposal.

Three directions

A · One panel, one number (recommended)
  • Pool as a chip at the top: "Kamino · USDC 5.03%"
  • Amount + TSLAx picker
  • Leverage chips 1.5× · 2× · 3× · 5× · Custom (default 2×), "Borrow 1,000 USDC → Kamino" under it
  • Headline: 4.2% APY · ≈ $42 a year, live
  • Risk line: ● 2.00 · Safe · liquidates if TSLA falls 90%
  • "Details" collapsed; sticky "Review" → bottom sheet → sign
B · Presets first
  • Three cards: Steady 1.5× · Balanced 2.5× · Boosted 5×, each with APY and health
  • Then just the amount, then the same review sheet
  • Easiest for newcomers; two screens; risks selling the 5× card
C · Strategy list as Farm home
  • Your open farms on top (value, health, earned)
  • Then one row per stock / pool ("TSLAx → Kamino USDC · up to 16.9%")
  • Tap opens A's panel. Use once there are several pools
RECOMMENDATION

Build A with B's preset chips; wrap it in C when more pools arrive. The long table goes: deposit repeats the input, borrow and "supplied to Kamino" become one line under leverage, the two APYs move to Details and the review sheet ("5.03% − 0.81% = 4.22%"), pool size and utilisation only appear as a warning when the pool is above 90% used, and projected profit becomes the live headline.
