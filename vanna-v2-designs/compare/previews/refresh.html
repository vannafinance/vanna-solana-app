3 · A Perps | Spot switch for Trade (owner's idea, like Lending | Spot)

What this is. On the web app's Portfolio, Lender has a Lending | Spot switch that changes what the page shows. The same idea for Trade: one switch at the top of Trade between Perps (leveraged markets) and Spot (swap any token). It is the standard pattern in trading apps: Binance and Bybit put Spot | Futures (or Derivatives) at the top of the trade screen, Coinbase Advanced has Spot | Futures, Hyperliquid has Perps | Spot (from my knowledge of these apps, not fresh research). What changes: Swap leaves the bottom bar, which becomes Markets · Positions · History; Spot shows the swap form in place; History already has Perps | Swaps, so the two match. What to watch: one switch per screen; positions stay Perps only (spot holdings are your assets on Portfolio).
