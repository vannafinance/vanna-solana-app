What changed in this version
  • Same screens and figures as V2 and V3. Only the navigation differs.
  • Modules under the logo. Portfolio, Earn, Trade and Farm are a glass bar inside the coloured header, right under the Vanna logo, with Swap as a round button in its middle. Each module's own pages are the bottom bar.
  • Nothing floats over the header's edge, so the content starts straight after the header. The bar is in the same place in every module.
  • Round 2, cleaner screens: under the header number, small chips replace sentences (P&L, open positions). Portfolio Overview opens with the Account health gauge from Profile. Every card shows its figures in one strip layout (label over value, equal columns). Markets is a table (Borrow APY, Liquidity, Price). Position cards, market details, Earn vaults and Farm cards are restructured the same way, and Farm history marks failed transactions.
  • Round 3: the Margin trade and Farm header now shows the account's whole value ($5,619.65), split into Net available and Borrowed. Below that: Health factor with its own Safe/Watch pill, Net leverage, and Room before liquidation with the collateral drop it allows. Overview keeps the health gauge and shows your top 3 positions. Lender and Trader are just Current and History. Profile no longer repeats Account health. Margin trade and Farm Positions open with a Net earnings chart and interest accrued. "Trade" is renamed "Margin trade". Each module now has its own colour: Portfolio the brand gradient, Earn teal, Margin trade violet, Farm rose.
  • Round 4: Portfolio wears the Vanna brand gradient (coral to violet). The Margin trade and Farm header shows the account value with a ring of Net available against Borrowed and the leverage inside it, then Health factor, Room before liquidation and how much of the account this module uses, the last two with a bar. Farm's pills are rose only (no coral), matching its header. Softer avatar ring; no light edge around the phone.
